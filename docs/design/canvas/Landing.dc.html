How it works

Judgement work, done before the bid is written.

Five steps, each one traceable to the tender and to your own documents.

01
Read
Upload the tender in any EU language. CrossBid extracts every requirement — exclusion, selection, technical, formal — each with a link to its source page.
02
Translate and explain
Each requirement is translated into your language and explained in plain terms, including what the buyer will accept as proof.
03
Map certificates
Requested certificates are matched to their equivalents in your home country through eCertis — Attestation URSSAF to Unbedenklichkeitsbescheinigung — with the legal basis.
04
Check evidence
Every requirement is checked against your company documents. Supported, partially supported, or unsupported — never assumed.
05
Verdict
Go, No-go, Conditional, or Insufficient evidence — with the compliance matrix behind it. A person confirms before anything proceeds.
